How to prepare artwork for printing

Start with the correct finished dimensions and keep important text away from the edge. Use high-quality images and check spelling, phone numbers and event dates.

Ask your printer to confirm their preferred file format, bleed, colour settings and resolution before exporting final artwork. These requirements may differ between suppliers.
