About PrintStart Adelaide

PrintStart Adelaide is a proposed printing startup concept for sole traders, microbusinesses and community event organisers in Adelaide.

Our goal is to make print choices easier to understand, including quantities, sizes and artwork preparation. Products and quote requests are presented for demonstration as part of a student project; no printing service or fulfilment is currently confirmed.
